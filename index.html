🏠 Our Story

Not a café.
A corner-shop for your whole mood.

Bingyu was born on busy streets, built for young people who want more than a drink — a place to browse snacks, snap photos, laugh too loud, and stay a while. Warm wood shelves, mural walls, giant cat statues and ceiling lights that make everyone look good.

From a “product window” to a youth lifestyle hub — 茶饮 + 零食 + 社交 + 休闲. That's the plan, and it tastes great.

  • 🪵 Warm wood + cream interiors — cozy, never cold
  • 🐱 冰语猫 everywhere: cups, murals, standees & merch
  • 📸 Photo-op corners designed for your grid
  • 🍿 Snack shelves for browsing between sips
